North Plaza to Room 201

Fictional Demo Hall. Step-free route via the west entrance ramp and Elevator A. The diagram is not to scale. Landmarks, distances and availability are simulated.

E to F connects the floors. Both letters mark a lobby for the same elevator.

Step-free directions

  1. A–B From North Plaza, follow the path beside the front steps up the west entrance ramp to the main doors. A push-button opener is available. 18 m
  2. B–C Pass through the inner lobby doors and continue straight to the west junction. 8 m
  3. C–D Follow the main corridor past the bulletin boards to the east junction. 25 m
  4. D–E Take the corridor to the Elevator A lobby on Floor 1. 10 m
  5. E–F Call Elevator A, enter and select Floor 2. Exit at the Floor 2 announcement. Length unrecorded.
  6. F–G Leave the elevator lobby and follow the corridor to the wide carpeted area at the central junction. 20 m
  7. G–H Cross the carpeted area to the door beyond the seating area. The raised-letter and braille sign marks Room 201. 6 m

The six segments with recorded lengths add up to 87 m. The elevator ride is additional.

If Elevator A is unavailable

With step-free routing on, the app reports “No suitable route found.” Stairs are excluded.
