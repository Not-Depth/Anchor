North Plaza to Room 201

Fictional Demo Hall. This route uses the front steps and Stairwell A. All places, arrival cues, distances and availability are simulated for team review.

Confirm the start, then each landmark in the app when you reach it. Your confirmation advances guidance. Choose Previous step if you confirmed too early. With spoken directions enabled, Repeat reads the current instruction and arrival cue again.

  1. Confirm the start

    Start at North Plaza (outside).

    Arrival cue. Open paved area with a bike rack and benches.

    Choose I'm at North Plaza (outside).

  2. Main Entrance lobby

    Walk up the five front steps to the main entrance doors. There are handrails on both sides.

    Arrival cue. Small lobby between two sets of doors, with a floor mat underfoot.

    Choose I've reached Main Entrance lobby.

  3. West junction, Floor 1

    Go through the inner lobby doors and continue straight until the corridor meets a cross corridor.

    Arrival cue. Corridors meet here; a drinking fountain hums against the wall.

    Choose I've reached West junction, Floor 1.

  4. Stairwell A door, Floor 1

    Continue straight across the junction to the end of the short corridor, where the Stairwell A door is.

    Arrival cue. Heavy door with a push bar; the stairwell echoes when it opens.

    Choose I've reached Stairwell A door, Floor 1.

  5. Stairwell A door, Floor 2

    Go up the stairs to Floor 2: two flights with a landing between them, handrails on both sides. Leave through the Floor 2 door.

    Arrival cue. Stairwell door with the floor number in raised letters on the frame.

    Choose I've reached Stairwell A door, Floor 2.

  6. Central junction, Floor 2

    Leave the stairwell and follow the corridor until it opens into the wide carpeted area.

    Arrival cue. Wide carpeted area with a seating area to one side.

    Choose I've reached Central junction, Floor 2.

  7. Room 201 (demo lab)

    Cross the carpeted area to the door on the far side of the seating area. That door is Room 201.

    Arrival cue. Door with a raised-letter and braille sign reading 201.

    Choose I've reached Room 201 (demo lab).

You have arrived at Room 201 (demo lab). Choose Finish to close guidance.
