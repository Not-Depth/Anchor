# Anchor working principles

1. Design the navigation flow for people who are blind or have low vision. Every route step needs usable text or speech. A drawing can illustrate the path.
2. Use authored nonvisual landmarks and let people confirm each arrival. Optional floor checks provide feedback and leave progression under the user's control.
3. Keep fictional demo data clearly labeled. Use surveyed evidence for claims about a real building.
4. Keep the iPhone app and web companion aligned where they share behavior. Describe camera-based LiDAR, recorded voice, and barometer checks according to the platform it runs on.
5. Evaluate obstacle sensing as an experimental aid. Measure actual audible timing, response and remaining clearance on an iPhone before declaring a device trial successful.
