# Anchor working principles

Draft for Ken's local feature work, 2026-11-05. Team ratification is open. These principles reflect the rules in the project README and team notes.

1. Preserve user control. Step-free routing uses confirmed suitable connections, excludes stairs and unavailable connections, and explains when no suitable route exists.
2. Keep provenance visible. Demo Hall is fictional. A real route needs its own survey record, with unknowns retained and a date and checker for verified facts.
3. Describe what the device senses. Nearby estimates distance in the center of the forward camera view. A front reading cannot establish that a surface is a wall or that the path behind someone is clear. Preserve missing-reading feedback, VoiceOver and independent audio/haptic preferences.
4. Match the evidence to the claim. Test routing and alert policy with reproducible inputs. Confirm actual speech timing and stopping clearance on a LiDAR iPhone. Record who reviewed a field decision before treating it as settled.

Update these principles against the team sources if a decision changes. Record the decision and its owner in the feature spec.
